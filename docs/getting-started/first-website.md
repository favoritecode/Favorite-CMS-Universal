# Creating Your First Website with Favorite CMS

Once installed, building your first website with Favorite CMS takes only a few minutes.

---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Dashboard

Navigate to `/admin` or `/admin/login` in your browser:
- **URL**: `http://favorite-cms.local/admin`
- Enter your administrator credentials created during installation.

The dashboard displays:
- **At a Glance**: Total counts of Published Posts, Pages, Categories, and Comments.
- **Quick Draft**: Rapid idea jotting without navigating to the full editor.
- **Activity Feed**: Recently published posts and user interactions.

---

## 2. Setting Up Site Identity

Navigate to **Settings &rarr; General**:
1. **Site Title**: Enter your brand or publication name.
2. **Tagline**: Describe your website's mission in a short sentence.
3. **Admin Email Address**: Used for recovery and system notices.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

---

## 3. Creating Categories & Tags

Before writing articles, organize your taxonomy:
1. Navigate to **Posts &rarr; Categories**.
2. Add main topics (e.g. *Technology*, *Design*, *Tutorials*).
3. Navigate to **Posts &rarr; Tags** to define secondary micro-topics.

---

## 4. Writing Your First Post

1. Navigate to **Posts &rarr; Add New**.
2. Enter an engaging headline.
3. Use the **Dual-Mode Editor**:
   - **Code Mode**: Format content with HTML toolbar buttons (Bold, Headings, Lists, Blockquotes, Code, Media).
   - **Preview Mode**: Review your exact typography in real-time as your visitors will see it.
4. Set a **Featured Image** using the integrated Media Library picker.
5. Select a **Category** and enter relevant **Tags**.
6. Click **Publish**.

---

## 5. Creating Static Pages (About, Contact)

1. Navigate to **Pages &rarr; Add New**.
2. Create an *About Us* or *Contact* page.
3. Set your content and click **Publish**.
4. Static pages immediately resolve at clean URLs such as `http://favorite-cms.local/about-us`.

---

## 6. Verifying the Frontend

Browse to the homepage `http://favorite-cms.local/`:
- The header displays your site title and tagline.
- Your published articles appear as responsive cards with reading time and category badges.
- Clicking any article opens the reading view with full featured image and discussion form.
