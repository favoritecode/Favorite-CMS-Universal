# Themes & Layout Customizer Guide

Favorite CMS Universal provides a clean separation between content, logic, and presentation. Visual appearance is governed by the Theme system (`/admin/themes`) and the live Layout Customizer (`/admin/customize`).

---

## 1. Managing Themes (`/admin/themes`)

1. Navigate to **Appearance** &rarr; **Themes**.
2. **Active Theme**: The top card displays the theme currently controlling your website (e.g. `Default Theme`).
3. **Available Themes**: Cards display installed themes with screenshot previews, version numbers, author details, and descriptions.
4. **Switching Themes**: Click **Activate** on any installed theme to immediately switch your site's presentation.
5. **Widget & Content Preservation**: Switching themes does not erase your widgets or database content. Region placements that match the new theme's declared regions are seamlessly retained.

---

## 2. The Theme Customizer (`/admin/customize`)

The Theme Customizer allows administrators to adjust branding and layout options with immediate preview:

### Layout Controls
- **Sidebar Position**:
  - **Right Sidebar**: Standard publication layout with main content on the left and widgets on the right.
  - **Left Sidebar**: Inverted layout with widgets on the left.
  - **Full Width (No Sidebar)**: Clean, distraction-free layout expanding content across the full container.
- **Container Width**: Select standard boxed (`1200px`) or expanded layouts.

### Branding & Colors
- **Site Logo**: Upload a custom logo graphic from the Media Library.
- **Brand Accent Color**: Hex color picker configuring links, buttons, highlighted badges, and active tab borders.
- **Header Background**: Light, Dark, or Custom brand colors.

### Homepage Section Management
Themes that support modular homepages allow you to enable, disable, and reorder sections:
- **Hero Banner / Slider**
- **Featured Articles Grid**
- **Latest Posts Feed**
- **Call to Action Block**
- **Newsletter Signup Strip**

Use the drag handles to reorder sections and click **Save Changes** to apply them to your live website.
